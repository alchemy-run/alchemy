---
title: Integrations
description: Auxiliary providers — databases, observability, CI — that compose with your primary cloud, managed as resources in the same Stack.
---

Integrations are providers that compose with your primary cloud:
databases, observability, and CI managed as resources in the same
Stack as your Workers and Functions. Register each one as a
`providers()` layer next to your cloud's, and alchemy handles
credentials, dependency ordering, and teardown across all of them.

- [PlanetScale](/integrations/planetscale) — serverless MySQL
  (Vitess) and Postgres with database branching.
- [Neon](/integrations/neon) — serverless Postgres with
  copy-on-write branching.
- [Axiom](/integrations/axiom) — observability: OTEL datasets,
  monitors, notifiers, and dashboards.
- [GitHub](/integrations/github) — repositories, Actions
  secrets/variables, webhooks, and repository event sources.

The primary platforms each get a full hub of their own — see
[Cloudflare](/cloudflare) and [AWS](/aws).
