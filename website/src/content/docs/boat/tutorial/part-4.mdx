## Deploy another stage

<PackageManagers run="alchemy deploy --stage staging" />

Each stage gets its own Worker and its own Boat key. Without `--stage`, you deploy to `live_$USER`.

## Share one Boat account

Stages share your Boat account. Sandboxes a stage's Worker creates carry the stage as a label (`alchemy.stage`), so you can list one stage's sandboxes without touching another's:

```typescript
const staging = yield* sandboxes.list({ labels: { "alchemy.stage": "staging" } });
```

Destroying a stage revokes its Worker's key and leaves its sandboxes to stop on their timers. Delete them when their owner goes away, as the example's `DELETE /users/:user` route does.
