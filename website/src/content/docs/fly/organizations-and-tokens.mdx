Everything you run on Fly belongs to an **organization**: its Apps and Machines, its volumes and databases, its private network, and its bill. Every account has a personal organization, and a team creates more and invites its members.

An organization is also a boundary. Its Apps share one private network, and nothing in another organization can reach them.

## A token decides the organization

Alchemy deploys with an **API token**. An organization token is scoped to one organization, so the token is how you choose where things go: a Service creates its App in the token's organization, and `Fly.App` uses it when you leave out `orgSlug`.

Create an organization token in the dashboard under **Account → Access Tokens**, or with the Fly CLI:

```sh
fly tokens create org
```

Then store it in a [profile](/environments/profiles):

```sh
alchemy profile edit --add Fly
```

See [Setup](/fly/setup) for the details.

## One organization per environment

A [stage](/environments/stages) decides what you deploy. The profile's token decides where. Give production its own organization, so its private network, its members and its bill are separate, and its own profile:

```sh
alchemy profile create prod
alchemy profile edit --profile prod --add Fly
alchemy deploy --stage prod --profile prod
```

Many stages can share a development organization, each with its own Apps.

## In CI

With `CI=true`, Alchemy skips the profile and reads `FLY_API_TOKEN` from the environment. Use a separate token for CI, so you can revoke it without touching yours. See [CI](/environments/ci).
